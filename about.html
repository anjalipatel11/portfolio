About Me

I'm Anjali, a designer who loves creating projects that balance usability, creativity, and technology. I recently graduated from the University of Maryland with a Bachelor of Science in Information Science and Psychology.

I've always been fascinated in understanding how people think, make decisions, and interact with technology. My undergraduate studies gave me the opportunity to explore the intersection of technology and psychology. Today, I am excited to apply that knowledge to designing digital experiences that exhibit both human and technical perspectives while being intuitive and accessible.

Whether it's conducting research or creating interfaces, I believe the best solutions appeal not only to the eye, but to the brain.

I've always been a creative person and that led me to realize my passion in digital design. Technology has been such a present part of my life and being able to combine that with a long-term artistic perspective has been amazing to explore.

People and technology have always fascinated me, two complex systems that continuously grow and change. Now having the opportunity to create something that intersects how people interact with technology is an even bigger and complicated system I am ready to tackle.
